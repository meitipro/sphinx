# Silent demo script

No voice. Every screen says what is happening; consensus waits are cut short with the transaction link left on screen.
Record at 1280 × 800 on https://sphinx-genlayer.vercel.app with two browser profiles (host and player wallets). Because
the shortest windows are one hour each, record in three sittings and cut them together.

| Time | Screen | What the viewer sees |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | Landing | The headline "Nobody copies. Nobody moves the answer." and the live riddle card with its sealed answers, only hashes |
| 0:08 | Host a riddle | Riddle, answer and notes typed in; Sealing 1, Reveal 1; Download ticket; Lock the answer and fund 10 GEN; the page opens the riddle |
| 0:22 | Riddle page, player wallet | Seal "a computer keybord": Connect wallet, Switch network, Get test GEN, Seal answer and pay; the list shows "sealed 9f2c…" and no answer |
| 0:40 | Riddle page, an hour later | Host: Reveal the answer and notes, "matches lock"; player: Reveal my answer |
| 0:55 | Riddle page, after the window | Judge the next ten answers: "Validators are judging ten answers", then the labels appear, CORRECT in orchid |
| 1:05 | Riddle #7 | A settled riddle: "Tastatur" and "a computer keybord" CORRECT, "a piano" CLOSE (hatched), "keyboard or piano" WRONG (struck), one seal never revealed |
| 1:12 | Claim | Claim on the player page; the claim transaction on the explorer |
| 1:20 | Docs, Evaluation | Golden cases 11 of 11 and held-out 3 of 3, every row with its transaction |
