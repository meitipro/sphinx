## What counts as correct?

An answer that names the same thing as the host's answer, as the host's notes define it. Spelling mistakes, other
languages, articles, synonyms and extra words are fine unless the notes say otherwise. See [The four labels](/docs/concepts/the-four-labels).

## Can I change my answer after sealing?

No. One seal per address per riddle, and the reveal must match it.

## What if I lose my ticket?

Load the downloaded file on the [player page](/me) or on the riddle page. Without the salt the seal can't be opened; the
answer is `UNREVEALED` and cannot win.

## Can someone copy my answer?

Not while sealing is open: only hashes are on chain. After sealing closes, reveals are public, but nobody can seal any more.

## What if the host vanishes?

Once the reveal window ends without the host's reveal, anyone can void the riddle. Every entry fee is refunded and the prize
is split among the players who revealed.

## What if nobody solves it?

Every entry fee is refunded and the host gets the prize back.

## Who sends judge?

Anyone, including you. It labels the next ten revealed answers in seal order. The call after the last batch settles the pot.

## Why do I have to claim?

`judge` and `void` only credit balances. On this network a payout must be funded at the root of its own transaction, so
each account claims its balance in one call.

## Which network?

GenLayer Studio Next, chain 61997. Test GEN comes from the site's faucet. See [Addresses and network](/docs/reference/addresses).

## Can a bot use it?

Yes. Every method is plain contract calls; the [Quickstart](/docs/quickstart) has JavaScript and Python samples that run
against the deployed contract, and [/llms.txt](/llms.txt) lists every docs page for agents.
