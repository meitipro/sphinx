Sphinx is a riddle and quiz contest on GenLayer. The host locks the answer and the notes on what counts before anyone
plays, players seal their answers so nobody can copy them, and after the reveal GenLayer validators judge every answer by
meaning, so typos, synonyms and other languages count. The prize pays the winners from the contract.

## The three things it fixes

Riddle contests and quiz nights break in the same three places.

- **Judging is exact or arbitrary.** A bot compares strings, so `keybord` loses and so does a correct answer in Spanish.
  A moderator decides by hand, slowly. On Sphinx several validators each judge every answer against the host's answer
  and notes, and an answer is paid only when they agree it is correct.
- **Answers are copied.** The first correct reply in a public channel is visible to everyone after it. On Sphinx only a
  hash goes on chain while sealing is open, and a seal includes the player's address, so a copied seal can never be
  opened.
- **Prizes depend on trust.** The host holds the prize and the answer and could change the answer after seeing the
  replies. On Sphinx the answer is locked by hash before the first seal, a different reveal is refused, and the prize sits
  in the contract until winners claim it.

## Lock, seal, reveal, judge, claim

<Steps>
  <Step>**Lock.** The host posts the riddle and a hash of the answer, notes and a salt, with the prize.</Step>
  <Step>**Seal.** Players seal a hash of their answer, a salt and their address, with the entry fee.</Step>
  <Step>**Reveal.** After sealing closes, the host and the players open their hashes in one window.</Step>
  <Step>**Judge.** Anyone sends `judge`: validators label the next ten answers in seal order, CORRECT, CLOSE or WRONG.</Step>
  <Step>**Claim.** Winners claim their share, or every fee is refunded if nobody solved it.</Step>
</Steps>

## What Sphinx is not

- **Not a trivia database.** Hosts write their own riddles and questions.
- **Not closed book.** Nobody can see what players look up, so hosts should write riddles, not lookups.
- **Not a lottery.** Only correct answers are paid; chance plays no part.
- **Not anonymous.** Answers are public after the reveal, next to the address that sealed them.

Everything here runs on GenLayer Studio Next, chain 61997, with test GEN. See [Addresses and network](/docs/reference/addresses).
