## The pot

The pot is the prize plus every entry fee, unrevealed seals included. It is shared in equal shares among the winners.
The indivisible remainder of that division, less than one wei per winner, goes back to the host.

## The two modes

| Mode | Winners |
|---|---|
| First N | The first N `CORRECT` answers in **seal order**. Seal order is when each answer was sealed, not when it was revealed or judged. |
| Split all | Every `CORRECT` answer. |

A `CORRECT` answer past the first N is recorded as correct, with its rank, and is not paid.

## When nobody is correct

Every entry fee comes back to its player, unrevealed ones included, and the host gets the prize back. A host can only ever
win back their own prize: an unsolvable riddle earns the host nothing.

## When the host never reveals

Once the reveal window ends without the host's reveal, anyone can call `void`. Every entry fee is refunded, and the prize
is split among the players who revealed (the remainder to the first of them in seal order). If nobody revealed either, the
prize goes back to the host.

## Credited, then claimed

`judge` and `void` only credit balances; they never move money. Each account then calls `claim` once, a top-level
transfer of everything credited to it, on every riddle. On consensus v0.6 a transfer can only be funded at the root of the
transaction that starts it, so payouts live in their own call. The [player page](/me) shows the balance and the button.
